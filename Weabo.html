VICTORY!!!!

Subaru is an amazing guy ( hehe ) who has inspired a lot of people that watched and/or read Re:Zero, including me

Im not sure what to say about the story other than it's just so damn good

And don't get me started on the freakin intro's man, the music is majestic

You are an amazing guy, Subaru
